# TAMIZH TECH ROBOTICS — PRODUCT & SERVICE HONESTY / FIT AUDIT

**Date:** September 2026  
**Auditor:** Antigravity Engineering & Product Audit  
**Target:** Tamizh Tech Robotics Company (`https://www.tamizhtech.in/`)  
**Objective:** Eliminate expectation mismatch, buyer confusion, false compatibility assumptions, and unsupported claims across all products, configurations, and engineering services.

## AUDIT EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Tamizh Tech Robotics operates a genuine multi-disciplinary ecosystem:
1. **Competition & Educational Robotics Products** (Robo Race, Robo Soccer, Line Follower 5.0, RC Transmitters, N20 & DGJ motors, Buggy Wheels).
2. **Manufacturing & Fabrication Services** (3D Printing in PLA/PETG/TPU, Precision Stainless Steel Laser Cutting in SS 304/316, PCB Design & Assembly).
3. **Engineering & Automation Solutions** (Industrial Automation, PLC/SCADA panels, Custom Robotics).
4. **Institutional Programs** (Schools STEM tinkering, Colleges Robotics CoE labs, Startup prototyping).

### Key Honesty & Clarity Vulnerabilities Identified (Pre-Audit)
- **High Risk (P0) — Competition Rulebook Disclaimers:** Previously, competition platforms like `rc-robo-race` and `rc-robo-soccer` did not prominently display a mandatory "Before You Enquire / Check Your Event Rules" warning, risking students ordering a 4WD buggy chassis for an event with strict dimension, motor, or wheel restrictions.
- **High Risk (P0) — Configuration Exclusion Transparency:** Products with variants (`Without Battery` vs `With Battery`, `Only Bot` vs `Full Kit`) did not explicitly list what is **excluded** and what setup hardware the customer must independently provide (e.g. LiPo charger, external 2.4GHz transmitter, battery connector soldering).
- **Medium Risk (P1) — Pricing Transparency:** Crossed-out "regular price" / "MRP" elements could be perceived as artificial promotional discounts rather than plain, honest catalogue pricing.
- **Medium Risk (P1) — Material Scope in Services:** Customers inquiring about laser cutting must immediately know that Tamizh Tech specializes **strictly in Stainless Steel (SS 304 & SS 316) sheet metal**, and does **not** cut wood, MDF, or acrylic.
- **Medium Risk (P1) — Service Timelines & Dependencies:** Turnaround times cannot be promised as fixed 24-hour windows because fabrication is dependent on CAD geometry, nesting, material stock, and machine scheduling.
